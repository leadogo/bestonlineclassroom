APPROVED ANSWERS (William, Oct 17 2026)

Q: How much does it cost? Is there a set-up fee, and what is the monthly?
A: It depends on your market area, the type of leads, and the cost per lead there, so that's exactly what the free call is for.

Q: How much should I spend on ads? What is a starting budget?
A: Depends on the market, but you don't need much. Plenty of people start at $10 a day.

Q: Is there a contract or lock-in? Can I cancel?
A: There are a few levels of commitment depending on what you need, each with its own options. We walk you through them on the call.

Q: How much of my own time does this take each day?
A: The part that's on you is talking to the leads, and that's 90% of the work. How long that takes each day is up to you.

Q: Is the call a hard sell? How long do I have to decide, and how long is this offer around?
A: No. William trains the team to be consultative on the call, the same way we teach you to be with your customers.

Q: Is there a guarantee or money back?
A: No guarantee, and be wary of any company that offers one, because they can't actually uphold it. Results with us work the same as with anyone, Zillow included: it comes down to how you handle the leads, and whether you're the one booking them.

Q: What results do people get? Conversion rates, hang-ups when they hear AI, do these leads close, does it work in a slow market?
A: Conversion runs 1% to 5%, same as any brokerage, team or market. Some people are fine talking to AI and some aren't, and that's okay. Yes, leads from this have closed. It still works in a slow market, it's just slower.

Q: Where can I see proof this works, other than your website?
A: Our website has dozens of testimonials, real agents talking at length with our team about using the system. William is an agent himself too. Beyond that, we hope what you've heard tonight makes sense.

Q: What exactly is this? Is Katherine a virtual assistant? What can she do, and is it only for real estate?
A: Yes. We work with residential real estate agents, and William goes through how it all works shortly.

Q: What does Katherine do on the calls? Outbound, texting, follow-up, warm transfer, contracts, speed to lead, and does she interrupt people?
A: She calls both ways, inbound and outbound, so she can answer the phone too. She also texts and emails. You set how often she follows up and the hours she works, and she does warm transfers. Contracts she doesn't do yet; that's a big feature still to build. Speed to lead, yes: she can answer within seconds. If you want her to interrupt people, you can turn that on. There's an advanced settings section where you choose things like how fast she talks and get as granular as you like about how she behaves.

Q: She sounds robotic or low energy. Are there other voices?
A: Over 5000 voices, male and female, and you can clone your own in 30 seconds.

Q: Are you AI? Do you have to tell people she is AI?
A: Lol, William is not AI. And yes, best practice is to tell people: some markets already require it, and going forward most likely all of them will.

Q: Does it work with my CRM (Follow Up Boss, KW Command, GoHighLevel, Compass, RealGeeks, Cloze, Maestro)? Do I have to buy one? Which do you recommend?
A: Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel, Lofty and KW Command all connect (Command goes through Zapier, and the team sets it up). If you don't have a CRM, Katherine can be it: contacts, stages, and an MCP server so you can hook her up to tools like Claude or ChatGPT, other CRMs, and email.

Q: Where do the leads come from? Facebook only, or Google, Instagram, TikTok too? What if people block ads?
A: Facebook and Instagram are our main lead sources. We sometimes run Google too, depending on the campaign.

Q: How does the pixel and the ad targeting work?
A: It runs on something called the Conversions API. Not the easiest thing to explain in chat, but it's machine learning that gets smarter the more data you feed it, so the more leads you get, the better the quality gets over time. The replay has a section on it, worth a rewatch.

Q: What do I need to set this up? My own Facebook account, a website, landing pages? Who does the setup?
A: You don't need your own website; the team can help with one. The pixel side is the Conversions API: learn it or hire someone you trust, and the call covers it.

Q: Is there a limit on how many leads I get?
A: No cap. It depends on how much you want to spend on ads, and that's your call.

Q: Does this work for a team, several agents or business names, or more than one market?
